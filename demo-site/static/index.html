What the seven gates check

  1. The K-1 document passes the standard's own validator
  2. Preparer-supplied IDs match the digits visible on the masked K-1
  3. Every K-1 value gets an outcome: sent, attested, or excluded by a named reviewer with a reason
  4. Answers the engine needs are present before it runs
  5. The engine accepted every entry
  6. No form was silently dropped during the calculation
  7. 1040 lines 2b, 3a, 3b, 7 and 8 reconcile back to the K-1 (±$1)

Synthetic data. The K-1 is the open standard's synthetic example; all IDs, attestations and reviewer decisions are invented. Nothing here is tax advice, and nothing is filed.
